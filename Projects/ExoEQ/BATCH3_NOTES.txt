Hide knobs that do nothing for the selected filter type.

ExoEQ.cpp (refreshKnobNames): a knob whose per-type label is "n/a" is now hidden (Hide(true)). Visible knobs are shown again on a type change.
Affected types (from KnobNamesForType):
- Z-Lowpass: Extra hidden
- Hermepass: Reso and Extra hidden
- SlewSonic: Reso and Extra hidden
- AverMatrix: Extra hidden

Known gap: the compact row keeps its 8 cells, so a hidden knob leaves an empty slot. Re-flowing the visible knobs into the freed space is a separate pass.
Not build-verified.
